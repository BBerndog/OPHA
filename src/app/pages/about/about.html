About Oak Park
Thank you for visiting the Oak Park community information website. Our OPHA community is located in Hendricks County, Guilford Township and the Town of Plainfield. The development is in the Plainfield School District as shown on the school district’s published information.

We pride ourselves in having a high-quality community that many consider a destination, and we manage the affairs of the development to ensure this standard is continued.

The development has 44 single family residences and is managed by the Oak Park Homeowners through homeowner volunteers who are responsible for management of the common areas and all administrative and legal processes. The OPHA management is done by a Board of Directors with officers selected from the Board. The President is Bill Munson, the Vice Presidents are Larry Edwards and Brad Bernhardt, the Treasurer is Wes Livingston and the Secretary is Linda Shore. We always appreciate new members joining the Board.

Homeowner participation in our community is very high, which is demonstrated by having a great turnout at our annual meeting which is held every year in July. This meeting is very important as this is where decisions are made about policies and our next budget. At the meeting we discuss the business of the community and review the budget to set dues for the next year. We welcome all homeowner input at the annual meeting, so if you are a resident (or plan on being a resident), plan on attending every July.
